Five Divisions. One World of Crafted Experiences.

Pricol Experia is the lifestyle and hospitality arm of the Pricol Group, bringing together Pricol Travel, Pricol Holidays, Pricol Gourmet, Dvara, and BluOrb under one vision. Backed by Pricol's 50-year legacy of innovation, trust, and excellence, Experia curates refined travel, leisure, hospitality, and gourmet experiences designed to enrich every journey and moment.

Established in 1975, the ₹5,000 crore Pricol Group is a diversified business enterprise headquartered in Coimbatore, with operations across five countries. Its interests span automotive technology and solutions, engineering, hospitality, corporate and leisure travel, logistics and packaging. With a workforce of over 12,000 people, the Group continues to build businesses driven by innovation, operational excellence and long-term value.
